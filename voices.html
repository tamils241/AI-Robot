REFERENCES

Check us, properly

Most vendors offer a happy customer. We would rather you spoke to the project where something went wrong.

Can we speak to a reference customer before we sign?

Yes, and we suggest you do it early. You get three calls at proposal stage, chosen by sector rather than by friendliness, plus a site visit to a system that has been running for at least six months.

Can we talk to a project that went badly?

We will give you that reference too. One pilot in 2024 missed its cycle-time target by 18% and we rewrote the programme rather than the claim; the customer speaks freely about both.

Are these results audited or self-reported?

The outcome figures are measured by the customer's own team against a baseline we agree in writing during discovery. Where a third-party audit exists, such as the Nordklinik safety case, we publish the certification reference.

Who from your team actually does the work?

The engineers named in your proposal. You meet them before you sign, they stay through handover, and their names appear on every weekly status report — no account manager relaying someone else's news.

What if the system misses the numbers in the proposal?

Then we have not delivered and you do not pay the final milestone. The targets, the baseline and the measurement method are written into stage one, so the argument is about data rather than memory.

Do you still support us after the contract ends?

Every system ships with a year of support, and about two thirds of clients extend it. The model, the simulator and the telemetry history are yours, so leaving us is a decision rather than a cliff.
