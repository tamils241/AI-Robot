PROCESS

From brief to
production line

Five stages, each with a defined exit criterion. You always know what is running, what it costs and what happens next.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Workshops, site audits and feasibility modelling. We quantify the value and the risk before writing a line of code.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    Digital twin first, hardware second. A working demo in weeks — not a slide deck in quarters.

  3. 03

    Build

    Modular hardware, versioned software, continuous integration and a simulation suite that runs on every commit.

  4. 04

    Deploy

    On-site commissioning, operator training, safety certification and a hypercare window with our field engineers.

  5. 05

    Evolve

    Fleet analytics feed the next model release. Your system compounds in capability every quarter.

FAQ

Before you brief us

The questions procurement and engineering ask us most often.

Do we have to commit to all five stages?

No. Discovery is a standalone fixed-fee engagement, and plenty of clients start with a single capability pilot. Stages two to five only follow once the previous exit criterion is met.

What do you need from us to start?

A named technical contact, access to the site or process data, and roughly two days of stakeholder time in the first week. We handle everything else, including measurement and safety paperwork.

Can you work alongside our existing integrators?

Regularly. We are comfortable being one supplier in a larger programme, and we hand over cleanly to in-house teams or to a local maintenance partner at the end.

How do you price the hardware?

Transparent, itemised and quoted before any purchase order. We do not take a margin on third-party components, so the number we quote is the number you pay.

What if the pilot fails?

You keep every artefact produced in that stage, including the code, the simulation and the data. Discovery fees are not refundable, but nothing else is owed.

How fast can we get started?

Discovery usually begins within two to three weeks of a signed NDA. If your programme has a hard date, tell us at enquiry and we will tell you honestly whether it is reachable.
